📌 Interac Cloud Access

Regarding the available access period for the INTERAC Cloud website, the User ID and Password can be used from 7:00 AM two days before the lesson until 10:00 PM on the day after the lesson.

Example: If the lesson is scheduled for September 8 at 10:00 AM, the User ID and Password will be valid from September 6 at 7:00 AM until September 9 at 10:00 PM.

📌 Meeting Link inside Cloud

The meeting link will only appear around 10 - 5 minutes before the lesson time.

Don't panic if the meeting link is still not at your cloud!!

your lesson does not required interac cloud,
